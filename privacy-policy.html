Privacy Policy for MedPrep

Last Updated: 27 October 2026

This is the single, current privacy policy for MedPrep on iPhone and Android. The apps link here rather than carrying their own copies.

1. Introduction

Welcome to MedPrep. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data in an open and transparent manner. This policy sets out what personal data we collect, who processes it, how long we keep it, how we use it, and your rights over it.

2. Data Stored With Your Account

  • Account Information: Your anonymous User ID, email address, and your name. Age is optional and only collected if you choose to contribute extra data.
  • Career and Progress: Your selected career tracks, the stations you complete, and the AI-generated feedback and scores you receive.
  • Experience Builder Content: Portfolio entries you create are stored with your account and used to tailor your feedback.
  • Onboarding Choices: Your answers to our onboarding questions, such as how you heard about us, your interview date and how often you want to practise.
  • Analytics and Crash Data: Metrics about how you use the app, such as which stations you open and finish, and crash reports including device type and operating system. These are linked to your anonymous User ID but not to your name or email.

This data is kept until you delete your account in the app, under Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete Account & Data.

3. Your Voice and Answers

Mock interviews

Your recording is sent to Google's Gemini models, through Google Cloud Vertex AI, to be marked. MedPrep does not store recordings on its servers. The copy on your phone is deleted once your feedback has been generated.

Live and conversation stations

On iPhone, your speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition. That text, and any replies you type, are sent to Google's Gemini models to play the other person in the scenario and to mark your answer, and the replies you hear are voiced by Google's text-to-speech services.

On Android, your voice is streamed to Google's Gemini Live model, which listens and replies in real time. A transcript of the conversation is then used to mark your answer.

Google may process this data outside the UK. Only the resulting feedback and scores are stored with your account.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To Provide the Service: To manage your account, give you access to interview stations, mark your answers and show your progress.
  • To Personalise Your Feedback: To tailor AI-generated feedback to your performance and to what you add in the Experience Builder.
  • To Improve the App and Support Research: Usage metrics and crash reports help us find bugs, see where people get stuck, and inform product development and research.
  • For Medical Education Research (with your consent): If you opt in during onboarding, your anonymised performance data may be used for research into better educational tools. You will not be identifiable.
  • To Contact You (with your explicit consent): If you opt in, we may email you about the service or invite you to a user research interview. Only users who have opted in are contacted. You can change this at any time in Settings → Contact me by email.

5. Service Providers

  • Google (Gemini via Vertex AI, text-to-speech): processes your recordings, transcripts and answers to generate feedback and role-play replies.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Crashlytics, Analytics, Remote Config): signs you in, stores your account data, and collects crash reports and usage metrics.
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, speech recognition, App Store): signs you in, transcribes speech in live stations on iPhone, and handles payments.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions. Your name, email address and purchase history are shared with them so we can identify your account for billing support and restore purchases. If you installed MedPrep from an Apple Search Ad, they also receive Apple's ad attribution data.

These providers process data on our behalf and do not use it for their own purposes. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else. Each provider has its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review.

6. Users Under 18

Medical school applicants may be under 18. Their voice, answers and account data are handled exactly as described above, with no profiling and no marketing use.

7. Data Storage and Security

Your account data is stored on Google Firebase servers. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to ask for a copy of what we hold, and to withdraw consent. You can manage your data and delete your account in the app's Data & Privacy settings, or contact us below. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will tell you about significant changes in the app or by email, and the date at the top of this page always shows the latest version.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, please contact us at Peter.Woods@doctors.net.uk.
